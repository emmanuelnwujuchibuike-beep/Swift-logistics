General
5 questions
What is Swift Freight Logistics?

Swift Freight Logistics is a full-service international freight and logistics company specialising in air, sea, road, and rail freight. We manage the entire shipping journey — from origin pickup through customs clearance to final-mile delivery — serving clients across 150+ countries worldwide.

Which countries do you serve?

We operate across 150+ countries on every continent. Our global network of partners, agents, and owned facilities allows us to reach even remote or restricted destinations. Contact our team for a route-specific assessment if you're shipping to an unusual location.

How do I get started with Swift Freight?

Getting started is simple:

  • Submit a quote request on our website with your shipment details
  • Receive a personalised quote within 24 hours from a dedicated specialist
  • Approve the quote and we register your shipment with a live tracking ID
  • We coordinate pickup, transit, customs, and delivery end-to-end
What types of freight do you handle?

We handle all major freight categories:

  • Air Freight — time-critical or high-value cargo
  • Sea Freight — FCL and LCL ocean shipments
  • Road Freight — domestic and cross-border trucking
  • Rail Freight — cost-effective long-distance overland routes
  • Special Cargo — hazmat, oversized, temperature-controlled, and high-security loads
Is my cargo insured during transit?

Yes. All shipments handled by Swift Freight are covered by our standard cargo liability policy. For high-value goods we strongly recommend comprehensive all-risk cargo insurance, which we can arrange on your behalf at a competitive rate. Ask your account manager for details when booking.

✦   Cargo insurance covers loss, theft, and damage from origin to delivery. Always declare accurate cargo value when booking.
Tracking
5 questions
How do I track my shipment?

Use the Track Shipment page and enter the SFL-XXXXXXXXX tracking number from your confirmation email. The dashboard displays real-time status, current location, checkpoint history, estimated delivery date, and any payment or customs holds on your shipment.

How often is tracking information updated?

Tracking data is refreshed every time your shipment passes a checkpoint — typically at origin pickup, departure hub, transit hubs, customs clearance, and final-mile handoff. For active shipments, you can expect updates every 12–24 hours on average, with more frequent updates near delivery.

My tracking shows no update for 2 days. What should I do?

Gaps in updates are normal during long ocean or rail legs where checkpoints are less frequent. However, if your shipment has not updated in more than 72 hours, please contact our support team with your tracking ID. We will investigate immediately and provide a status report within 4 hours.

What does "Customs Hold" mean on my tracking?

A customs hold means your shipment is undergoing inspection by the destination country's customs authority. This is a standard procedure for international freight. In most cases no action is required from you, and the hold is resolved within 2–5 business days. We will notify you by email if any documentation or duties are required.

Can I share my tracking link with a recipient?

Yes. Your tracking page URL (swiftfreightlogix.netlify.app/payment.html?id=SFL-XXXXX) can be shared with anyone — recipients, business partners, or customs agents. The page is publicly accessible with your tracking ID and shows all status information in real time.

Shipping
5 questions
What items are prohibited from shipping?

We do not ship items that are illegal under international law or restricted by destination country regulations. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Narcotics and controlled substances
  • Weapons, firearms, and explosives
  • Counterfeit goods and pirated materials
  • Currency, negotiable instruments, or bearer bonds
  • Live animals without proper permits
  • Perishable goods without temperature-controlled arrangement
✦   Hazardous materials may be shipped under specific IATA/IMDG certifications. Contact us for a compliance assessment.
What are your packaging requirements?

Cargo must be properly packaged to withstand international transit. For fragile or high-value items, we recommend professional crating or export-grade cartons. All packages must be clearly labelled with the recipient's full name, address, and our tracking ID. Packing lists and commercial invoices must accompany all international shipments.

How long does delivery take?

Transit times vary by mode and route:

  • Air Freight — 1–7 business days
  • Sea Freight (FCL) — 10–30 days depending on route
  • Road Freight — 2–14 days within region
  • Rail Freight — 10–20 days (Europe–Asia corridors)

Customs clearance can add 2–5 business days. Your quote will include an estimated delivery window.

Do you handle door-to-door shipments?

Yes. We offer full door-to-door service including origin pickup, packing assistance, export documentation, freight, customs clearance at destination, import duties management, and final-mile delivery. You can also choose port-to-port or door-to-port arrangements for partial service.

Can you ship oversized or project cargo?

Yes. Our project cargo division handles heavy lifts, breakbulk, out-of-gauge (OOG) cargo, and industrial equipment. We coordinate specialised vessels, flatbed trailers, cranes, and port operations. Contact our team for a bespoke project logistics plan.

Payments
4 questions
What payment methods do you accept?

We accept multiple payment methods to suit clients globally:

  • Wire bank transfer (SWIFT/IBAN)
  • Bitcoin (BTC)
  • USDT (TRC20 and ERC20)
  • Approved corporate credit accounts (for registered businesses)
✦   Payment details are provided in your secure tracking dashboard and confirmation email. Never send payment to any address not verified through our official system.
When is payment required?

Payment terms depend on your service arrangement. For new clients, freight charges are typically required before dispatch or at a defined transit milestone. Established corporate accounts may qualify for 30-day net payment terms. Any outstanding balance must be settled before final delivery is released.

My shipment shows "Payment Required" — what do I do?

Open your tracking page and scroll to the Payment section for the exact amount due and accepted payment methods. Complete payment and send the transaction reference to swiftfreightlogix@gmail.com with your tracking ID. Your shipment will resume within 24 hours of payment confirmation.

Are there hidden fees?

We operate with full pricing transparency. Your quote includes all known charges: freight, fuel surcharges, handling, and documentation. Import duties, destination customs taxes, and port storage fees are the responsibility of the consignee and may not be known in advance. We will notify you immediately if any unexpected charges arise during transit.

Support
4 questions
How do I contact customer support?

You can reach our support team through multiple channels:

  • Live chat on any page of our website (bottom-right corner)
  • Email: swiftfreightlogix@gmail.com
  • Contact form on our Contact page

Our support team operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

What is your response time?

Live chat responses typically arrive within minutes during active hours. Email and contact form enquiries receive a response within 24 hours. Urgent shipment-related issues (delays, customs holds, lost cargo) are escalated and addressed within 4 hours regardless of time zone.

What if my shipment is lost or damaged?

If you believe your shipment is lost or damaged, contact support immediately with your tracking ID. We will open a formal investigation within 24 hours. Claims for lost cargo must be submitted within 14 days of the expected delivery date. Damage claims must be reported within 48 hours of receipt with photographic evidence. Our insurance team will guide you through the claims process.

Can I modify or cancel my shipment after booking?

Modifications (address changes, weight updates, service level changes) are possible before cargo is dispatched from origin. Once in transit, changes may incur additional fees. Cancellations before pickup are generally accepted without charge. Post-dispatch cancellations are subject to our freight recovery policy. Contact your account manager as soon as possible to discuss your options.
