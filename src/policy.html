01

Introduction

Swift Freight Logistics ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our freight and logistics services.

By using our services, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you disagree with any part of this policy, please discontinue use of our website and services.

Our Commitment

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes. Your data is used exclusively to provide and improve our logistics services.

02

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us and information collected automatically when you use our services.

Information you provide:

  • Contact information (name, email address, phone number, company name)
  • Shipment details (cargo type, weight, origin, destination, declared value)
  • Billing and payment reference information (we do not store full payment credentials)
  • Communications you send us via contact forms, email, or chat
  • Account registration details if you create a business account with us

Information collected automatically:

  • IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs
  • Pages visited, time spent, and clickstream data
  • Device identifiers and mobile network information
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Cookies section)
Data Controller
Swift Freight Logistics
Legal Basis
Contract performance, legitimate interests, consent
03

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to operate, improve, and personalise our services. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Process and manage your freight shipments and generate tracking IDs
  • Communicate shipment status updates, delays, and delivery confirmations
  • Respond to your enquiries, quotes, and support requests
  • Process billing, issue invoices, and manage payment verification
  • Send service announcements and operational notifications
  • Improve our website, services, and customer experience
  • Comply with legal obligations, customs requirements, and regulatory filings
  • Detect, prevent, and respond to fraud, security incidents, or abuse

We will only use your personal information for the purposes listed above, or for compatible purposes that you would reasonably expect based on your relationship with us.

04

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your data in the following limited circumstances:

  • Logistics partners: Carriers, port agents, customs brokers, and local delivery operators require shipment and consignee information to complete your delivery
  • Customs authorities: We are legally required to submit consignee information, cargo declarations, and commercial invoices to customs in origin and destination countries
  • Service providers: Trusted vendors who assist with email delivery, website hosting, analytics, and payment processing — bound by confidentiality agreements
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, court order, or government authority to protect rights, property, or safety
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may transfer to the successor entity
05

Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • TLS/HTTPS encryption for all data transmitted to and from our website
  • Encrypted storage for sensitive personal and business information
  • Access controls limiting staff access to personal data on a need-to-know basis
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Secure, access-controlled infrastructure hosted on enterprise-grade cloud platforms

While we implement robust security measures, no system is completely impenetrable. If you believe your data has been compromised, contact us immediately at swiftfreightlogix@gmail.com.

06

Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use the following categories:

  • Strictly Necessary: Required for the website to function — session management, security tokens, theme preferences
  • Functional: Remember your preferences (e.g. dark/light mode, language) across visits
  • Analytics: Aggregate data on how visitors use our site — we anonymise this data before analysis

Most browsers allow you to manage or block cookies in their settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some features on our site.

07

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data. We honour these rights regardless of jurisdiction:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data where it is no longer necessary for its purpose
  • Portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Restriction: Request that we restrict processing of your data in certain circumstances
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time where processing is consent-based

To exercise any of these rights, email swiftfreightlogix@gmail.com with your request. We will respond within 30 days.

08

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Typical retention periods:

  • Shipment and transactional records: 7 years (tax and audit compliance)
  • Contact and enquiry records: 3 years from last contact
  • Newsletter subscriptions: Until unsubscribed
  • Website analytics data: 26 months (anonymised after 14 months)

After retention periods expire, data is securely deleted or anonymised.

09

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for business and commercial use by adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 16. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child without parental consent, we will take steps to delete that information promptly. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we may also notify you via email or a notice on our website. Your continued use of our services after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.
